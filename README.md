# Growth Audit — Zeely

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/zeely-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for Zeely's iOS onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype of the flow rebuilt in Zeely's own design. Only the order of screens changes. The art direction stays yours.

Open the prototype directly: https://bterzic123.github.io/zeely-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give people a second chance when they close the paywall, using your own 2-Month Plan ($85.99) | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask for the password after the purchase, and keep the name question | Name → Account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put their goal in the paywall headline ("350 customers a month by Dec 27") | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for tracking and notifications after the paywall | Welcome | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Let the loader read their answers back | Loader | CR +10–15% |

Run together: **CR to purchase +25%, ARPU +30%.**

## Grounding

Every price, rating, review and feature line comes from Zeely's own published material: the App Store listing and its in-app purchase list (US storefront: 3-day trial then $19.99/week, 3-Month Plan $119.99, 2-Month Plan $85.99; 4.6 from 23K ratings), zeely.ai, and Zeely's in-app screens (2,000,000+ businesses, the loader reviews, the "What you get" features). Photography is cropped from Zeely's own screens.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Zeely.
